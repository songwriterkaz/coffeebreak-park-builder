Coffeebreak Park Builder
Open index.html in a browser (or play online). Walk with WASD or the joystick, jump with Space or Jump.
Build, Rotate, Place, Delete, Music, Music on or off, Shop, Rewards, Send money, Outfit, My Park and Done are at the bottom.
Your park saves on this device automatically. Install app adds it to the home screen. It also works offline after the first visit.
Made by Coffeebreak.
The Tool Shop has 12,000 coin items. Create a character, then join; other players appear in the park in real time.
Coins have no cash value and can't be exchanged for money.
